Your first 15 minutes with RIA.

Everything below works before any approval, because it only uses the public, fictional-data sample report. The times are a suggested order, not a promise that you will finish in exactly 15 minutes.

0–3 min

Understand the sample

Open the engine-generated sample report. The recording data is wholly fictional, no installation is needed, and analyzer version 0.7.58 produced it.

3–6 min

Find one candidate finding

Pick the highest-severity finding and read its timestamp, its severity and what the analyzer claims was observed.

6–10 min

Follow the evidence

Open the linked observations and the event timeline. Write down one piece of evidence that supports the finding and one thing the recording does not contain. The guided walkthrough does this with three recorded incidents if you would rather be led.

10–12 min

Understand the limits

Say out loud why this candidate is not an automatically confirmed physical fault. If that is clear, you are reading the report the way it is meant to be read.

12–15 min

Decide your next step

Three honest options: prepare an evaluation request, stay with the public sample, or stop here. None of them requires a payment.

If you are approved for the private pilot

Access is manual and nothing is downloaded from this site. After approval, the delivery channel and evaluation terms are agreed separately.

  • Windows: the unsigned portable ZIP is checked against the SHA-256 you receive, then extracted and run as RobotIncidentAnalyzer.exe. It is not an installer and Windows may warn about an unsigned file.
  • Python: run the provided zipapp on Python 3.10 or newer.
  • Data: analyze only recordings you are permitted to use, on your own machine. Nothing is uploaded by the analyzer.
The private evaluation package is a separate 0.7.59 build. Its output may differ from the 0.7.58 sample report.

When something goes wrong

SituationWhat to do
The sample report does not openOpen the link again in a current browser, or download the file and open it locally.
Timeline interactions do not respondCheck that JavaScript is enabled; the report is interactive and static-only browsers show the text without the timeline.
You do not have a GitHub accountYou can still build and copy a draft, but the current verified submission channel requires GitHub. No other channel is verified yet.
Windows blocks the portable buildThe build is unsigned. Verify where the file came from and its SHA-256. Do not disable security features on our advice.
A ROS message type is not supportedSome formats or optional dependencies may be unsupported. Check the evaluation guide for the supported scope.
No anomalies were detectedThat does not prove the robot behaved correctly; signals may be missing from the recording. It is a coverage question, not a clearance.
