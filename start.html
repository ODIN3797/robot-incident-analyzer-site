The warehouse robot in our reel is a joke. Robot Incident Analyzer (RIA) is a real research-stage tool for robotics engineers who need to review incident evidence from recorded ROS2 bags and telemetry logs.

Our social videos and on-screen error messages are AI-generated fiction. They are not real robot failures or actual RIA diagnosis results.

What RIA does

Go from scattered telemetry to evidence you can review.

ROS and robot telemetry inputs

Inspect supported ROS2 bag files and timestamped robot event/telemetry CSVs using an offline workflow.

Timeline and review findings

Compare observed events and signals, flag candidate anomalies, and jump to supporting timestamps.

Shareable reports

Generate HTML/JSON investigation outputs. Rules identify review candidates—not automatically verified root causes.
