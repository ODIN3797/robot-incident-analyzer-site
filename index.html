01 / THE PROBLEM

When the robot stops,
the questions start.

A pile of recordings is not a diagnosis. Find the relevant seconds, compare what the robot was told to do with what it actually did, and make the next investigation step clear.

⌁

Hours of scattered traces

Commands, odometry, sensor states and warnings rarely tell a complete story in isolation.

◎

Too many places to look

Jumping across topics makes it easy to lose the time window that matters.

◈

Uncertain conclusions

A signal mismatch is evidence to inspect, not proof of a particular hardware failure.
