Your investigation workflow

From raw recordings to a reviewable case.

01 · IMPORT

Analyze locally

Open your authorized MCAP, ROS 2 DB3, ROS 1 BAG, or CSV recording. The pilot runs on your computer without uploading files to our servers.

02 · TRIAGE

Prioritize anomalies

Inspect candidate events like velocity shortfall, stale diagnostics, topic gaps, and sensor inconsistencies. Missing coverage is surfaced instead of hidden.

03 · REVIEW

Share evidence

Open an HTML investigation report with context windows and aligned signals, and export one incident for a focused engineering conversation.

Designed for honest diagnostics

Evidence, not invented root causes.

The analyzer flags potential anomalies for a human to review. Observed correlation is not proof of physical cause. Public simulation and synthetic examples have been exercised; independently confirmed field-fault precision and recall have not yet been established.
